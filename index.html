About

Dr. Lamin E. Drammeh is Associate Vice President for Public Service and Agriculture and Director of Strategic Initiatives, Evaluation, and External Affairs at South Carolina State University. His career spans K-12 and higher education, food and agricultural systems, and international development.

He has helped secure and implement more than $90 million in grants and cooperative agreements from U.S. federal agencies, African national governments, the World Bank and other multilateral donors. He is Co-Principal Investigator on a historic $70 million USDA Partnership for Climate-Smart Commodities award to SC State and Clemson University, supporting South Carolina farmers.

Dr. Drammeh leads strategic planning and work plan processes for SC State's 1890 Research and Extension program. He builds partnerships across the United States, sub-Saharan Africa and Latin America, and applies data-informed evaluation to institutional decisions, budget forecasting and SACSCOC compliance.

Beyond the university, he is one of SC State's most decorated athletes: track captain, NCAA All-American, inductee of the SC State (2012) and MEAC (2021) Halls of Fame, and a competitor for The Gambia at the 1996 Atlanta Olympic Games.

USAID Textbooks and Learning Materials Program

As Program Manager of this flagship Africa Education Initiative program, he coordinated the development, publishing and distribution of 3.4 million biology, chemistry, physics and mathematics textbooks for Tanzania. For nearly two decades it was the largest grant in SC State's history.
